# Hex tiles

**Fantasy Hex Tiles** by The Clover Patch — https://cuddlyclover.itch.io/fantasy-hex-tiles

Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: the sprite sheet (`fantasyhextiles_v3.png`) was cut into one image per tile and scaled up 4× with
nearest-neighbour sampling. The cave tiles are the cave overlays drawn onto hill, snow and canyon tiles.
The `*-blight.png` tiles are grayscale versions (for shadowblighted regions) and `uncharted.png` is a
sepia version of the steppe tile. See `tools/slice-tiles.py`.
